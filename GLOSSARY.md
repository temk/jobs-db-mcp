# Job Search

A personal store of job postings collected from external sites, scored and reviewed to decide
where to apply.

## Language

**Job**:
A single job posting on a source site, identified by the site and the site's own id.
_Avoid_: Vacancy, position, posting

**Digest**:
A short Russian summary of a job's required and preferred qualifications, five to seven
sentences, for skimming without reading the full description.
_Avoid_: Summary, abstract

### Scores

**Fit score**:
How well the CV matches what the job asks for, 0 to 100. The employer's view.
_Avoid_: Match score, relevance

**Interest score**:
How attractive the job is to the candidate (role, company, domain, location, conditions), 0 to 100.
_Avoid_: Relevance, priority

### Management level

**Management level**:
How much people management a job involves. Unknown until determined.

**Individual contributor**:
No reports.
_Avoid_: IC-only, engineer

**Tech lead**:
Leads technically, with no formal reports, or with reports the posting leaves unclear.

**Team lead**:
Manages a small team and stays hands-on.

**Group lead**:
Manages a large team or other managers.
_Avoid_: Manager of managers, director

### Work mode

**Work mode**:
Where the work is done: on-site, hybrid, or remote. Unknown until determined.

### Status

**Status**:
The outcome of the candidate's review of a job. Set by the candidate, never derived from scores.
Every status except New is final: a reviewed job is not revisited.

**New**:
A job not yet reviewed.

**Unsuitable**:
A job the candidate will definitely not apply to.
_Avoid_: Rejected, not interested

**Low priority**:
A job kept as a last resort, considered only if nothing better works out.

**Applied**:
The candidate has sent an application. What happens afterwards is not tracked. Answers whether
the candidate has already applied to that employer.

**Closed**:
A job found withdrawn at review time, before the candidate applied.

**Duplicate**:
A job that is the same position as another stored job, for example the same opening posted by
the employer and by an agency, or reposted under a new id.

### Publishers

**Organization**:
An employer or an agency, under one canonical name shared by all its jobs. The same
organization may act as an employer for one job and as an agency for another.

**Direct job**:
A job published by its employer, with no agency in between.

**Employer**:
The organization the hired person will actually work for.
_Avoid_: Client, company

**Agency**:
An intermediary that publishes jobs on behalf of an employer, often without naming it. An
application sent to an agency is not guaranteed to reach the employer.
_Avoid_: Recruiter, placement company
